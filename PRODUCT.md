# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Owners and managers of United States local, appointment-driven businesses. They evaluate Nafa while handling customers, working in the field, or covering after-hours demand; their job is to stop losing calls and leads without taking on another system to manage.

## Product Purpose

Nafa Technologies designs and deploys AI call agents, instant lead follow-up, and conversion-focused websites. The public website turns qualified visitors into booked discovery calls, with a free quote as the secondary path.

## Positioning

Nafa combines the capture layer (website), immediate outbound response, and 24/7 inbound call handling into one done-for-you growth system, built directly with the client rather than routed through an outsourced intermediary.

## Operating Context

Visitors compare service providers on desktop and mobile, often outside normal office hours. They need to understand the offer quickly, hear or see a demonstration, and choose between booking a meeting or requesting a tailored quote.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The site is a Next.js 15 and Tailwind application. It includes service, industries, pricing, case study, blog, demo, contact, legal, and thank-you routes plus quote, schedule, lead-card, mobile CTA, and chat interactions. Public pricing is intentionally withheld. Existing customer proof and performance claims are placeholders or unverified unless explicitly sourced.

## Brand Commitments

Nafa's name, supplied logo, and the promise "Never Miss a Lead. Never." remain. The user explicitly requested a dark redesign with a high-quality 3D perspective-grid or halo background inspired by the supplied reference image. The voice is direct, capable, and free of empty AI jargon.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains the product requirements document, brand logo, real feature descriptions, a simulated interactive demo, and starter blog content. It does not contain confirmed client logos, testimonials, case-study results, founder imagery, or a live CRM/Calendly integration.

## Product Principles

- One obvious path to a conversation: book a meeting.
- Demonstrate automation as calm, dependable coverage rather than novelty.
- Say less, prove more, and never repeat a claim merely to fill a section.
- Keep every service and supporting resource discoverable without crowding the primary navigation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, keyboard-visible interaction states, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion alternatives. The dark visual field must be decorative and never required to understand content.
